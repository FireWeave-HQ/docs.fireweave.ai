---
title: "Guardrails"
description: "The metrics a rollout watches, their limits, and what happens on a breach."
---

A guardrail is a metric from your observability tool, a limit, and what to do when the limit is crossed. Fireweave reads each guardrail while a stage holds, and acts on a breach.

| Metric | Limit | On breach | Latest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `checkout_errors` | is above 5 | **Block** | Healthy |
| `p95_latency_ms` | is above 800 | **Alert** | Healthy |
| `checkout_started` | is below 100 | **Info** | Not reported yet |

<Note>The **Guardrails** tab on each rollout shows this table. The values above are an example.</Note>

## On breach

| Setting | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Block** | Parks the rollout at 0% and sends a Block alert. |
| **Alert** | Sends an alert; the ramp waits for a person. Exposure stays. |
| **Log** (shown as **Info** in the table) | Records the reading. Never acts. |

To ramp automatically, a rollout needs at least one **Block** metric, and a **Minimum events** on every **Alert** and **Block** metric.

## How readings count

- A breach counts only after two readings in a row cross the limit.
- **Minimum events** sets the fewest events a reading needs before Fireweave judges the metric. A reading with fewer events never breaches.
- On an automatic rollout, a stage moves on only after every Alert and Block metric reads healthy three times.
- A stage where no metric reports holds the ramp and sends an alert. An outage at your observability tool is not read as a bad release.

## Where guardrails come from

Your coding agent records what to watch with the change, so the rollout arrives with its guardrails. Metrics are read from the observability tool connected to the environment. See [Observability](/connect/observability).

## Add or change a metric

Anyone with Write on the project can change metrics until the rollout ends. On the rollout's **Guardrails** tab, press **Add metric**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the metric">Search what your vendor reports, or type the exact name your app emits.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the limit">Choose **Breaches when** (is above, is below, is at least, is at most) and a **Limit**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose On breach">**Block**, **Alert** or **Log**. Optionally set **Minimum events**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save metric">It counts in the current stage within 5 minutes.</Step>
</Steps>

### Custom metrics

A metric your app hasn't sent yet, such as one this change adds, isn't in the vendor's list. Type its name to add it as a custom metric. It reads **Not reported yet** and never breaches until the first reading arrives.

<Tip>**Check now** reads every metric immediately without changing the rollout.</Tip>
