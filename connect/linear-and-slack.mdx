---
title: "Linear, Slack and Custom HTTP"
description: "Link a Linear project, add your Slack workspace, and call your own endpoint before a rollout promotes."
---

These tools are optional. Add each account once in **Settings › Integrations**; adding one needs an Owner or Admin, or a role with **Manage integrations**.

| Tool | What Fireweave does with it | Where you set it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Linear** | Shows the linked project's issues on your rollouts. | Project **Settings › Integrations** |
| **Slack** | Connects your Slack workspace through Fireweave's Slack app. | **Settings › Integrations** |
| **Custom HTTP** | Calls your endpoint as a promotion check before a rollout step promotes. | Project **Settings › Promotion checks** |

## Linear

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Linear account">
    An Owner or Admin presses **Add Linear account** and signs in to Linear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the project">
    In your project's **Settings › Integrations**, pick one Linear project. Any Member can do this once the account exists.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Linear is set for the whole project. Observability is set per environment: see [Observability](/connect/observability).

## Slack

Add your workspace from **Settings › Integrations › Slack**. You sign in to Slack in a new window to choose the workspace.

## Custom HTTP

Add a **Custom HTTP** account for your endpoint, and store any credential it needs as a named secret on that account, never in the check. How the check answers and when it runs: [Strategies and checks](/ship/strategies-and-checks).
