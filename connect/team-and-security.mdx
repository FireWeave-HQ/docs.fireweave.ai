Everyone in your organization has one role, and every role covers every project. Manage people in **Settings › Members** and roles in **Settings › Roles**.

| Role | Projects | What it can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full access | Everything, including ownership and deleting the organization. An organization has one Owner. |
| **Admin** | Full access | Members, settings, keys, access and every project. |
| **Member** | Write | Creates projects and works in every project. |
| **Viewer** | Read | Reads every project and changes nothing. |

## Custom roles

Owners and Admins create a role with **New role**. Give it a name, a project access level (**Read** or **Write**), and any of these organization permissions:

| Permission | Lets them |
| --- | --- |
| **Invite members** | Invite people, only to roles no wider than their own. |
| **Manage integrations** | Add and remove accounts for tools. |
| **Manage CI keys** | Create and revoke the organization's CI keys. |
| **View audit log** | Read **Settings › Audit log**. |

## Invite people

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite">
    In **Settings › Members**, press **Invite people**, paste the emails and pick a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    Each person opens the invite email and confirms their address with a 6-digit code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invites last 7 days. Resend, copy the link or withdraw one from **Invitations**. To hand over the organization, pick **Transfer ownership** from a member's menu in **Members**. They have 7 days to accept, and you become an Admin.

## Your sign-ins

**Account › Security** shows where you're signed in, and lets you sign out of each:

| Section | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign-in methods** | Email and password, and Google. |
| **Browsers** | Where you're signed in on the web. |
| **CLI devices** | Where `fw` is signed in as you, in each organization. |
| **AI apps** | AI tools you let read FireWeave for you. They can read, not change. |

**Revoke everywhere** signs out every other browser, CLI device and AI app. This browser stays signed in.

## For Owners and Admins

| Page | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Settings › Audit log** | What Owners and Admins changed, and who did it: members, keys, integrations and settings. |
| **Settings › CI keys** | Every CI key, and members' `fw` devices. Sign out a device there; its owner runs `fw login` to sign in again. |
